IELTS Study Hub

Practice resources for IELTS, sorted by skill and resource type, so you can find what you need right now.

A note on sources: There's a lot of unofficial IELTS material online — leaked papers, third-party mock tests, app-based quizzes. Difficulty and accuracy vary a lot. Treat the official resources and the Cambridge IELTS series (1-19) as your main calibration for difficulty, and use the other sites mainly to get familiar with question types rather than trusting their scoring.
